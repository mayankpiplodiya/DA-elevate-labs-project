Insights:

The Real-Time Public Sentiment Dashboard analyzes live tweets to understand overall public opinion by classifying them into positive, negative, and neutral sentiments. It enables real-time tracking of trends, allowing businesses to observe how public perception changes instantly in response to events, product launches, or news. This helps in identifying brand perception and detecting potential crises early, especially when there is a sudden rise in negative sentiment.

The system also reflects the effectiveness of marketing campaigns through increases in positive sentiment and provides valuable customer feedback by analyzing user opinions shared on social media. Neutral sentiment plays an important role by representing informational or undecided views, which can be further analyzed for deeper insights. Additionally, the dashboard highlights frequently discussed topics, helping identify areas of high public interest and engagement.

The system is scalable and capable of handling continuous tweet streams while updating the dashboard automatically. Overall, it supports better decision-making by providing real-time, data-driven insights that help organizations improve their strategies, products, and customer experience
