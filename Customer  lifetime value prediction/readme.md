Insights:

1. Customer Spending is Highly Concentrated

A small percentage of customers contribute a disproportionately large share of total predicted revenue, confirming the presence of high-value customer segments that significantly drive profitability.

2. Frequency Strongly Influences Lifetime Value

Customers with higher purchase frequency tend to have significantly greater predicted LTV, indicating that repeat engagement is a key driver of long-term revenue growth.

3. Recency Impacts Future Revenue Potential

Customers who have purchased recently show higher predicted LTV compared to inactive customers, highlighting the importance of continuous engagement and retention strategies.

4. Average Order Value (AOV) Differentiates Premium Customers

Higher AOV customers consistently fall into the top LTV segments, suggesting premium product buyers or larger transaction users represent strong monetization opportunities.

5. Predictive Modeling Improves Marketing Precision

Using XGBoost regression enables accurate estimation of customer value, allowing businesses to shift from reactive to data-driven targeting strategies.

6. Segmentation Enables Targeted Resource Allocation

By dividing customers into Low, Medium, and High LTV segments, marketing budgets can be allocated efficiently toward high-return customer groups.

7. Retention Strategy is More Cost-Effective Than Acquisition

Since high LTV customers contribute a major share of revenue, retaining them yields greater ROI compared to acquiring new low-value customers.

8. Early Identification of High-Potential Customers

The model helps identify medium-segment customers who show behavioral patterns similar to high-value customers, enabling proactive upselling and loyalty campaigns.

9. Low LTV Segment Represents Growth Opportunity

Customers in the low segment may not currently contribute significant revenue, but targeted incentives and personalized engagement can improve their lifetime value.

10. Data-Driven Decision Making Enhances Strategic Planning

The integration of machine learning predictions with Google Sheets dashboards provides actionable insights for business teams, enabling strategic planning based on measurable customer value rather than assumptions.
